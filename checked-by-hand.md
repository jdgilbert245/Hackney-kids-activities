    # Checked by hand

Details confirmed by a person that the agent can't find online, e.g. by phoning a venue or reading a noticeboard. The agent uses each one until its recheck date, then asks for it to be checked again in its weekly pull request.

One line per fact: what it applies to, the fact, when it was checked, and when to recheck. A term or six months is a sensible recheck period.
